# SatBhoomi Score + Raksha — Underwriting Suggestion Rules (T-07, FR-006)

This document specifies the deterministic business logic and agronomic rules for **Task T-07 (Loan Suggestion Rules)**, implementing **FR-006** of the Product Blueprint.

---

## 1. Governance & Core Guardrails

1. **Rule 1: Human-in-the-Loop Decision Making**
   - SatBhoomi **NEVER approves or rejects a loan**.
   - All outputs generated by this engine are structured **decision-support recommendations** to assist human bank loan officers.
   - The final credit decision, sanction limit, and disbursement authorization rest exclusively with the loan officer.
2. **Deterministic & Zero-PII**
   - All calculations are pure mathematical functions of plot agronomic attributes (crop, operational area, irrigation method, risk score/band, and existing insurance status).
   - Zero personally identifiable information (PII) is consumed or produced.
3. **Graceful Handling of Incomplete Observations**
   - If a plot assessment reports `status="insufficient_data"`, the engine suspends automated sizing and returns `status="manual_appraisal_required"`, preventing ungrounded lending decisions.

## 2. Scale of Finance (SoF) & Gross Credit Sizing

In agricultural lending in Gujarat, loan limits are anchored to the **Scale of Finance (SoF)** determined annually by the **District Level Technical Committee (DLTC)** for each crop and irrigation regime.

### 2.1 Applied Rates for Rabi Wheat (Anand District)

| Crop | Irrigation Method | Scale of Finance (₹ / acre) | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wheat** | Irrigated (`tube_well`, `canal`, `drip`, `sprinkler`) | **₹32,000** | Standard DLTC baseline for certified seed, fertilizer (NPK/DAP), power/fuel, and labor across irrigated alluvial plains. |
| **Wheat** | Rainfed | **₹22,000** | Reduced operational input investment without assured tubewell irrigation. |

### 2.2 Gross Scale Calculation & RBI Collateral-Free Ceiling

$$\text{Gross Scale Limit} = \text{Operational Area (acres)} \times \text{Scale of Finance (₹/acre)}$$

Per Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Kisan Credit Card (KCC) directives:
- The maximum collateral-free agricultural lending ceiling is **₹2,00,000**.
- If $\text{Gross Scale Limit} > \text{₹2,00,000}$, the base for right-sized smallholder loan sizing is capped at **₹2,00,000**, and an explanatory advisory note is attached.

## 3. Risk-Based Right-Sizing (Haircut Multipliers)

To protect both the financial institution from default and smallholder farmers from over-indebtedness, SatBhoomi applies deterministic limit haircuts based on the calibrated farm risk band:

$$\text{Suggested Limit Band} = \left[\text{Capped Scale} \times \text{Ratio}_{\min},\; \text{Capped Scale} \times \text{Ratio}_{\max}\right]$$

Limits are rounded to the nearest ₹500 for practical banking disbursement.

| Risk Band | Calibrated Score Range | Haircut Multipliers | Recommended Action / Underwriting Posture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Risk** | $0 \le \text{Score} \le 34$ | **90% – 100%** | **Full Scale Limit**: Strong vegetative vigor and low historical stress. Standard disbursement with minimal credit friction. |
| **Medium Risk**| $35 \le \text{Score} \le 69$ | **65% – 80%** | **Cautious Limit**: Moderate historical or in-season vegetative drag. Limit right-sized to cover essential working capital while buffering against repayment stress. |
| **High Risk** | $70 \le \text{Score} \le 100$ | **40% – 50%** | **Conservative Limit**: Compound moisture or vegetative stress. Tight cap requiring branch manager review and structured field verification. |

## 4. Harvest-Aligned Repayment Schedule

Smallholder agricultural loans frequently fail due to rigid monthly EMI structures that ignore crop marketing cycles. SatBhoomi aligns repayment with harvest cash realization:

- **Crop Season**: Rabi Wheat
- **Harvest Window**: March – April
- **Peak APMC Mandi Arrivals**: April – May
- **Scheduled Repayment Due Date**: **May** (Bullet Repayment)
- **Economic Rationale**: Eliminates intra-season liquidity pressure during crop growth, allowing farmers to liquidate wheat grain at local mandis during the peak price window before loan maturity.
- **High Risk Advisory**: For plots in the High Risk band, the engine recommends a mid-season physical field inspection during heading (February/March).

## 5. Crop Insurance (PMFBY) Rules

SatBhoomi links risk assessments directly to the **Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)**:

| Current Insurance Status | Evaluated Risk Band | Mandatory Flag | Insurance Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Already Insured (`insured=1`)** | Any Band | `False` | Plot is verified as already covered by crop insurance. |
| **Uninsured (`insured=0`)** | **Low Risk** | `False` | PMFBY enrollment recommended to hedge unseasonal weather events, but not mandatory. |
| **Uninsured (`insured=0`)** | **Medium Risk** | `True` | Mandatory PMFBY enrollment required prior to loan disbursal to mitigate elevated farm stress. |
| **Uninsured (`insured=0`)** | **High Risk** | `True` | Mandatory PMFBY enrollment required prior to loan disbursal to mitigate elevated farm stress. |
| **Any (`status="insufficient_data"`)** | Insufficient Data | `True` | Mandatory field appraisal and PMFBY enrollment required due to data gaps. |
